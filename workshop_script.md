# EVA Workshop Script

## Introduction (Friend's Part)

- Welcome and brief introduction to the workshop.
- Overview of the importance of ethical decision-making in software engineering.
- Introduction to EVA as a tool to practice and improve these skills.

## Part 1: Tool Guidance (Your Part)

"Thank you [Friend's Name]. Now, I'll walk you through how to use EVA. Our goal today is to familiarize you with the platform and its features, so you can confidently use it to practice your ethical reasoning."

### Step 1: Landing Page and Logging In

"First, let's navigate to the EVA landing page. Here, you'll find a brief overview of what EVA does. For this workshop, we have already created accounts for you. Please use the credentials provided to log in."

- **Action:** Direct users to the login page.
- **Visual:** Show the landing page and then the login screen.

### Step 2: The Dashboard - Your Mission Control

"After logging in, you'll arrive at the main dashboard. On the left, you'll see the main navigation panel, which includes access to conversations, practice modules, and ethical guidelines."

- **Action:** Briefly explain the layout of the dashboard.
- **Visual:** Have the main dashboard open.

### Step 3: Understanding Your Manager Type (Optional)

"Before we dive into a scenario, some of you might be prompted to take a 'Manager Type Quiz.' This is a short quiz to help you understand your own managerial tendencies when faced with ethical dilemmas. It's a great way to gain some self-awareness before you start practicing."

- **Action:** If applicable, guide users through the quiz. If not, you can skip this.
- **Visual:** Show the quiz modal.

### Step 4: The Core of EVA - Interactive Practice Scenarios

"Now for the main event. The core of EVA is the interactive practice module. Here, you'll be presented with realistic workplace scenarios where you'll have to make tough ethical choices."

- **Action:** Guide users to the practice module.
- **Visual:** Navigate to the practice module on screen.

"You will be interacting with an AI-powered manager persona. There are three types:

1.  **The Puppeteer:** A manager who will directly pressure you into unethical actions.
2.  **The Camouflager:** A manager who will disguise unethical requests as normal business.
3.  **The Diluter:** A manager who will acknowledge your concerns but downplay their importance.

Today, we'll all start with the [Choose a starting scenario/manager type] scenario."

- **Action:** Instruct everyone to start the same scenario.
- **Visual:** Show the scenario selection screen.

### Step 5: Engaging in the Scenario

"As you go through the scenario, you will be presented with choices. Read the situation carefully and choose the response that you feel is most appropriate. There are no right or wrong answers, but your choices will influence the outcome of the scenario and the feedback you receive."

- **Action:** Let users engage with the scenario for a few minutes.
- **Visual:** Show a scenario in progress.

- **Action:** Point out the knowledge panel.
- **Visual:** Open and showcase the knowledge panel.

### Step 6: Receiving and Understanding Feedback

"After you complete a scenario, you'll receive detailed, AI-generated feedback on your performance. This feedback will highlight your strengths, suggest areas for improvement, and analyze your reasoning."

- **Action:** Give users time to review their feedback.
- **Visual:** Display the feedback screen and walk through its different components.

### Step 7: The Tactics and Fallacies Guide

"One last feature I'd like to highlight is the 'Tactics and Fallacies Guide.' This is a built-in reference to help you identify rhetorical tactics and logical fallacies that you might encounter in ethical discussions. It's a valuable resource for becoming a more effective ethical advocate."

- **Action:** Show users where to find this guide.
- **Visual:** Open the guide and briefly explain its contents.

## Part 3: Q&A and Wrap-up

"That was a quick tour of EVA. I encourage you to continue exploring the different scenarios and features on your own. Now, I'd like to open the floor for any questions you might have."

- **Action:** Facilitate a Q&A session.
- **Visual:** Your face! :)

"Thank you all for your participation. We hope you find EVA to be a valuable tool in your professional development."
